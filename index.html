It started in 2017 as a hobby — a camera, no plan, just documenting whatever was in front of me. At the same time I was heading into NUS College of Design and Engineering to study Materials Science and Engineering, where I learned to break complex problems into their components and solve them with systematic rigour — the same way you'd approach a materials failure or a stress analysis.

Still an undergrad, I shot my first commercial job in 2021 — for a client I'm still shooting for today. The engineering mindset that got me through NUS turned out to be exactly what made me a precise photographer too — I think in light the way I used to think in load paths.

I graduated in June 2022 and joined Micron Technology as a Product Integration Engineer that July. Eight months in, I was laid off as part of a global workforce reduction — a hard reset I didn't choose.

"The layoff didn't end anything. It forced the pivot I needed."

By March 2023 I'd started private tutoring in Science and Maths. The ability to reverse-engineer complexity — to find the underlying structure of a hard topic and explain it from first principles — turned out to be exactly what tutoring required. Seven months later, in October 2023, I joined Educare4u to add centre-based teaching alongside my private students.

In March 2024, I formally registered Accurova with ACRA — giving a name and a studio to three years of commercial shoots already in motion. It's since delivered 900+ shoots and a Singapore SME500 Award in 2026.

Then in December 2025, an 11-month relationship ended, and I suddenly had a lot of time and a lot to process. I put it into code. The same workflow frustrations I'd felt as a creative and a tutor — repetitive edits, disorganised files, manual processes — were everywhere, and I started building tools to solve them: Telegram bots, AI-powered pipelines, RAW processing engines. TheBooleanJulian ramped up fast, born from shipping real things that real people use.

In March 2026, I joined BNI Crescendo as an active member and chapter photographer — the network side of a practice that's always run on real relationships.

Today, the three strands are one practice: precision, innovation, and the belief that craft without execution is just potential.

Common Questions

FAQ

What types of photography do you specialise in?
Corporate events, cosplay and character portraits, astrophotography, maternity, lifestyle, and product shoots. Each discipline applies the same FRAME method — but the technical approach is calibrated to the specific shoot type. If you're unsure whether your shoot fits, just ask.
Do you travel for shoots?
Yes, within Singapore without any surcharge. For shoots outside Singapore (including Malaysia and the region), travel and accommodation costs are discussed upfront and factored into the quote. The NUS Astronomical Society astro trips to Tioman and Langkawi confirmed that the camera goes wherever the shot requires.
How quickly are photos delivered?
Standard delivery is within 7–14 days of the shoot, depending on the volume of selects. Priority delivery (faster turnaround) is available on the Premium and Full-Day packages. Every client receives all raw selects to review before final edits are confirmed.
Can you help with AI workflow integration or automation for my business?
Yes, through TheBooleanJulian. This covers Telegram bot development, AI pipeline integration using the Claude API, workflow automation, and Python scripting. If you have a repetitive process eating your team's time, there's almost always a way to automate it. Reach out via LinkedIn to discuss.
What levels and subjects do you tutor?
Primary 3 to Primary 6 Mathematics and Science, and Secondary level Physics and Chemistry (O-Level syllabus). Available both privately (Upteach GreatGrade — 1-on-1, flexible location) and through Educare4u tuition centre. If you're unsure whether your child's level is a fit, a diagnostic session can be arranged first.
How do I book a session or get a quote?
For photography: visit accurova.com/contact or message via Instagram @accurova. For tutoring: reach out via LinkedIn or the contact links below. For development work: connect on LinkedIn with a brief description of the project. Response time is typically within 24 hours on weekdays.
